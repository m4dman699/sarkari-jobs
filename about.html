About this website

Government recruitment information, organized in one place

SarkariJobs+ is an independent information website that organizes public government recruitment notices, application dates, admit-card updates and results. It is not a government website, recruiting authority, or partner of the organizations whose notices are listed.

Our purpose is to help readers find the recruiting organization, the dates available in a notice, and a route to the relevant official source. We do not accept applications, issue admit cards, publish official results, or decide candidate eligibility.

How listings are prepared

Public listing feeds and recruitment portals help us discover updates. Information is organized into SarkariJobs+ pages and may be refreshed automatically. The source organization and official notice link are shown when available. A third-party listing can help locate an update, but it is not the recruiting authority.

Dates and other facts can change or be missing from a portal's short listing. Always check the latest notice on the recruiting authority's own website before paying a fee, applying, downloading a document, or relying on an eligibility detail.

Corrections and questions

For a correction, include the SarkariJobs+ page URL and the corresponding official notice link. Please do not post application credentials, identity documents, or other private candidate information.
