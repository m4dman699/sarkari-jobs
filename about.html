What SarkariJobs+ is

SarkariJobs+ is an independent Indian information website. We organize government job notifications, admit-card updates and result announcements into one searchable tracker so candidates can find a listing quickly, understand its key dates in plain language, and finish their work on the recruiting authority's own portal.

We are not a government website, and we do not remember users: there are no accounts, no logins and no charges. Every page here is free to read, and the final step — applying, downloading, or verifying — always happens on the official website linked from the listing.

What you will find on every listing page

  • An original SarkariJobs+ summary of the listing, written in our own wording from the record's facts.
  • A key-facts table with the dates and limits shown in the source — anything the source does not state is left as “Not stated in the source”.
  • A status banner that tells you at a glance whether applications are open, closing soon or closed; whether a hall ticket is out; or whether a result has been declared.
  • A date timeline of application, exam and result milestones when the source provides them.
  • A short “How to use this page” guide and frequently asked questions.
  • One clearly labelled block of official links, and a source-attribution block naming where each record came from.

How listings are prepared

Our pipeline runs automatically once a day. It collects records from official portals, and also uses long-running public listing indices — such as SarkariResult.com and FreeJobAlert.com — to discover notices we might otherwise miss. Those index sites are independent businesses with no connection to SarkariJobs+; each page credits where its record came from.

We restate every listing in SarkariJobs+ wording. We do not copy a notice's text, and we do not guess: missing dates, fees or vacancy counts stay visibly unfilled. When a source cannot be re-read during a scheduled run, the record keeps its last good data rather than being dropped.

What we will never do

  • Accept applications, documents or fees on anyone's behalf — that only happens on official portals.
  • Publish expected dates, “leaked” question papers or unofficial answer keys presented as facts.
  • Present another website's index page as if it were the recruiting authority's portal.
  • Ask readers to create an account to read anything on this site.

Corrections

Spotted a wrong date, a broken link, or a listing that should have been withdrawn? Tell us through the contact page — the public issue tracker is the only channel we use, so every report stays visible and auditable. Verified corrections are applied in a scheduled update.

Our sourcing and wording rules are set out in the editorial and content policy, and what happens to reader data is described in the privacy policy.
