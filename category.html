Every job notification in the current feed, grouped by the category recorded in its source notice. Each card opens a summary with the dates the authority published and a link to the official page.

How a government job recruitment works

  1. Official notice with dates and eligibility
  2. Application before the last date
  3. Written or computer-based test
  4. Merit list or result published
  5. Verification and appointment

This is the general process, not a promise for any one post. Every date, fee and eligibility clause comes from the official notice linked on the listing.
