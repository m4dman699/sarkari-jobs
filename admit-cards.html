The process

How a government job recruitment works

This is the general process, not a promise for any one post. Every date, fee and eligibility clause comes from the official notice linked on the listing.

  1. Official notice with dates and eligibility
  2. Application before the last date
  3. Written or computer-based test
  4. Merit list or result published
  5. Verification and appointment

Before you travel

Exam-day checklist

Check your own admit card for the exact documents, reporting time and centre. This is a general reminder, not a substitute for the recruiting authority's instructions.

  1. Download the hall ticket from the official portal
  2. Keep a printed copy and a phone copy
  3. Carry the photo ID named in the notice
  4. Reach the exam centre before the reporting time
  5. Follow only the instructions on your admit card
