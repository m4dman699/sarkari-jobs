Editorial standards

How we source and present recruitment updates

SarkariJobs+ is independent and is not affiliated with the Government of India, any state government, or a recruiting authority. Our pages are informational summaries; the recruiting authority's current notification is the source to rely on.

Sources and links

  • We prioritize official recruiting authority portals and notices for application, admit-card and result links.
  • Third-party job portals may help us discover a notice. We identify the recruiting organization where known and direct readers to an official source when one is available.
  • We do not present another publisher's page as an official notice or claim that its wording or data is our exclusive content.

Dates and missing details

We show dates when a source provides them. If a deadline, fee, eligibility rule, vacancy count, or other fact is not available or cannot be confirmed from the source information, the page should direct readers to the official notice instead of guessing. Recruitment authorities can revise or withdraw a notice after it is listed here.

Updates and corrections

Listings may be refreshed by automated feed updates. Automated collection can miss a change or misread a page, so readers should verify details with the authority before acting. To report a specific error, send the SarkariJobs+ page and official notice link through the project's GitHub issues page ↗.
