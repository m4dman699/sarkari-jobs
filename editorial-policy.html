Editorial and content policy

🗓 Effective 23 October 2026✅ Official-portal-first👀 Public corrections tracker

Policy at a glance

Primary sources
Recruiting authority portals, official notices and result PDFs
Discovery support
Long-running public index sites (e.g. SarkariResult.com, FreeJobAlert.com), always credited on the page
Update cycle
Automated pipeline once a day at about 02:17 UTC; sitemap dates move only when a page actually changes

Editorial independence

SarkariJobs+ is an independent information site. It is not owned by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any government body, and the sites credited in our records (such as SarkariResult.com and FreeJobAlert.com) have no connection to us. We choose, describe and correct listings at our own discretion. We do not accept payment to feature, promote, or hide any listing.

What we publish

Each tracking page summarizes one public recruitment event — a job notification, an admit-card release, or a result/recruitment update — using structured fields we can point back to a source. Pages are labelled by type, and the official links block is clearly separated from our guidance so readers never mistake an index site for the recruiting authority.

Sourcing hierarchy

  1. Official first. When the recruiting authority's portal or notice is reachable, we take the record's facts from there and link it directly.
  2. Discovery via credited indices. If the authority page is unreachable by our automated run, a long-running public listing index may be where the record is discovered. In that case the index is recorded and shown as the fact source on the page, and the official portal remains the link readers are directed to.
  3. Never copy. Whatever the source, we recheck the facts against the record and write the page in SarkariJobs+ wording. Credited index sites are not affiliated with us and get no endorsement either way.

Original content rule

Every page's summary, guidance, FAQ and layout are written for SarkariJobs+. We do not reproduce notice text, index-site pages, or any other website's wording wholesale. Facts are stated only when present in the source; missing items stay visible as “Not stated in the source”. We never invent dates, vacancy counts, fees, deadlines or links — not even “expected” ones.

Automation disclosure

The collection and publishing pipeline runs automatically once a day, at about 02:17 UTC. It reads sources, retains the last good data when a source cannot be re-read, regenerates listing pages, and updates the sitemap only for pages whose content actually changed. Corrected records reported through our tracker are re-verified and applied in a scheduled update by a human editor.

Corrections and removal

Readers can report a wrong date, a broken or wrong link, or a withdrawn listing through the contact page. Corrections are reviewed between daily updates and valid fixes go out in a scheduled build. When a listing is officially withdrawn, we remove it or clearly mark it closed with the official notice linked.

Copyright and takedown

Our pages are original summaries of publicly published facts, with the source credited on every page. If you are a rights holder and believe a page infringes your copyright, send a takedown request through the contact page naming the page and your rights basis. We act promptly on valid requests and remove or amend the material while we verify.

Advertising policy (as of today and for the future)

SarkariJobs+ does not currently display advertising, sponsored posts or paid links. If we later enable advertising (for example, after ad-network approval), it will be clearly separated from editorial content, never bundled into listing facts or official-link blocks, and labelled. We would not accept advertising that impersonates government portals, sells jobs or documents, or targets this site's readers with solicitations inside our pages.

Questions about this policy

Policy questions, privacy matters and listing corrections all go through the contact page. Background on the site is on the about page, and reader data practices are in the privacy policy.
