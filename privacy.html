Privacy policy

Your privacy on SarkariJobs+

🗓 Effective 23 October 2026📊 Aggregate analytics only🚫 No accounts, no forms

Privacy at a glance

Accounts
None — every page on this site is readable without signing up
Personal data requested
None — we never ask for names, phone numbers, documents or payment details
Advertising cookies
None today; if advertising starts later, consent will be collected first for EEA/UK visitors

Overview

SarkariJobs+ publishes free information about government job notifications, admit cards and results. You can read every page without creating an account or giving us any personal information. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we have no data to sell: readers leave nothing but anonymous traffic signals.

What we never collect

  • No names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses or dates of birth.
  • No résumés, identity documents, mark sheets or payment information — job applications only ever happen on official portals we link to, not here.
  • No user-generated content, comments or uploads.

Analytics and cookies

We measure aggregate traffic with Google Analytics 4. Analytics cookies and browser storage are used only to understand how many people read a page and from where in general terms — pages viewed, approximate region, device type and referrer. Google's privacy terms govern this processing, and IP addresses are not used to build readable user profiles.

You can prevent analytics entirely: block cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's official opt-out add-on ↗. Declining analytics does not limit any feature of this site.

Advertising and consent

This site currently shows no advertising and sets no advertising cookies. We plan to apply for an advertising service (such as Google AdSense) in future, to keep the service free. Before any personalized advertising reaches readers in the EEA/UK, we will add a certified consent-management platform and honour its choices ahead of any ad scripts — and, if admitted, publish an ads.txt file at the site root. Any ads would be clearly separate from listing content and official links, as set out in the editorial policy.

External links

Listing pages link to recruiting authority portals and credit the sources of each record. Other websites — including the official portals — have their own privacy practices, which we do not control and are not responsible for. Apply only through official pages, and never share documents or personal details on any third-party or “agent” site.

Sharing and hosting

This site's code and data are deployed through our hosting/deployment providers purely to serve the pages you read. Analytics data is processed by Google as described above. Nothing else is shared, and no personal-data-based decisions are ever made about readers because we hold no personal data.

Retention and removal requests

Analytics data is retained for the period configured in the analytics service. Because we publish information from public notices, a notice may occasionally contain personal details. If you are named in a listing and want information reviewed, send a request through the contact page naming the page; we review it against the official record and act as appropriate.

Children

This site is a general information service and is not directed at children under 13. We knowingly collect no personal information from any visitor, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy is dated and updated when practices change; the current version is effective 23 October 2026. Questions can go through the contact page.
