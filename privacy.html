Privacy policy

This policy explains what SarkariJobs+ does with information when you visit sarkarijobsplus.com.

Information and analytics

This is a public information website. It does not currently ask visitors to create accounts, submit job applications, or make payments. Google Analytics 4 is used to understand site usage. It may collect page views, scrolls, outbound-link clicks, device and browser information, referral information, and online identifiers such as cookies, subject to Google's settings and policies. We do not intentionally ask visitors to enter sensitive personal information into this site.

Google processes Analytics data on our behalf. Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy ↗. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and can use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on ↗.

Advertising

SarkariJobs+ does not currently display third-party advertisements. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled in the future, Google and other ad vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on visits to this or other sites. Visitors can manage Google's personalized-ad settings at Google Ads Settings ↗. We will enable ads only after publishing any additional disclosures or consent choices that apply.

External websites

Links can take you to government recruiting portals and other external websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those site operators, not SarkariJobs+. Review the destination site's policy before sharing information or applying.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when our data practices change. For a privacy question, contact the project through GitHub issues ↗. Do not include passwords, identity documents, or candidate application details in a public issue.
