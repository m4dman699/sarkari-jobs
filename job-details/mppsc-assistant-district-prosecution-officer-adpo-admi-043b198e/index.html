How to use this page

This page tracks the admit card release for Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission covering candidates in Madhya Pradesh. Hall tickets are issued only by the exam authority, so download from the portal linked above and check every printed detail before the exam day.

Never share registration numbers, passwords or date of birth with third parties promising to download a hall ticket for you — carry the official printout instead.

Frequently asked questions

How do I download the admit card?

Open MPPSC's official portal from the links section above and sign in with your registration or roll number and date of birth, exactly as used during the application.

What should I check on it?

Confirm the printed name, photograph, exam date and shift, centre address and category. Report any mismatch to MPPSC through its official contact channels before the exam day.

When is the examination?

The listing records the exam date as 1 November 2026. Reach the centre per the shift stated on the admit card, not on any third-party website.
