UPSSSC PET Admit Card 2026 is an examination update from Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC). Confirm the exam schedule and candidate instructions on the authority portal before downloading.

Key details

Eligibility
Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Service Selection Commission (UPSSSC), has released the Admit Card for the examination of Preliminary Eligibility Test UPSSSC PET on its official website. The exam will be conducted on 25 October, 30 October & 01 November 2026. and the last date to submit the application form was 07 September 2026. How to use this page

If you applied for this exam, verify your slot and centre on the official hall ticket from the authority portal. Any discrepancy in the printed details should be reported to UPSSSC well before the exam date.

⚠️

Never share registration numbers, passwords or date of birth with third parties promising to download a hall ticket for you — carry the official printout instead.

Frequently asked questions

How do I download the admit card?

Open UPSSSC's official portal from the links section above and sign in with your registration or roll number and date of birth, exactly as used during the application.

What should I check on it?

Confirm the printed name, photograph, exam date and shift, centre address and category. Report any mismatch to UPSSSC through its official contact channels before the exam day.
