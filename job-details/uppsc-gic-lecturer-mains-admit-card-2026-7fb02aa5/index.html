If you applied for this exam, verify your slot and centre on the official hall ticket from the authority portal. Any discrepancy in the printed details should be reported to UPPSC well before the exam date.

How do I download the admit card?

The listing records the exam date as 30 August 2026. Reach the centre per the shift stated on the admit card, not on any third-party website.
