How to use this page

Staff Selection Commission SSC has released these call letters covering candidates in Delhi. Keep a printed copy with a valid photo ID ready for the exam, because entry depends on the details printed on the card, not on any forwarded copy or text message.

Never share registration numbers, passwords or date of birth with third parties promising to download a hall ticket for you — carry the official printout instead.

Frequently asked questions

How do I download the admit card?

Open SSC's official portal from the links section above and sign in with your registration or roll number and date of birth, exactly as used during the application.

What should I check on it?

Confirm the printed name, photograph, exam date and shift, centre address and category. Report any mismatch to SSC through its official contact channels before the exam day.

When is the examination?

The listing records the exam date as 18 December 2025. Reach the centre per the shift stated on the admit card, not on any third-party website.
