Frequently asked questions

How do I download the admit card?

Open CBSE's official portal from the links section above and sign in with your registration or roll number and date of birth, exactly as used during the application.

What should I check on it?

Confirm the printed name, photograph, exam date and shift, centre address and category. Report any mismatch to CBSE through its official contact channels before the exam day.
