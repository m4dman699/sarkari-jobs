Frequently asked questions

How do I download the admit card?

Open UPESSC's official portal from the links section above and sign in with your registration or roll number and date of birth, exactly as used during the application.

What should I check on it?

Confirm the printed name, photograph, exam date and shift, centre address and category. Report any mismatch to UPESSC through its official contact channels before the exam day.

When is the examination?

The listing records the exam date as 3 November 2026. Reach the centre per the shift stated on the admit card, not on any third-party website.
