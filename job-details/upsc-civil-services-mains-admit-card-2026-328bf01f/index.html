How to use this page

If you applied for this exam, verify your slot and centre on the official hall ticket from the authority portal. Any discrepancy in the printed details should be reported to UPSC well before the exam date.

Never share registration numbers, passwords or date of birth with third parties promising to download a hall ticket for you — carry the official printout instead.

Frequently asked questions

How do I download the admit card?

Open UPSC's official portal from the links section above and sign in with your registration or roll number and date of birth, exactly as used during the application.

What should I check on it?

Confirm the printed name, photograph, exam date and shift, centre address and category. Report any mismatch to UPSC through its official contact channels before the exam day.

When is the examination?

The listing records the exam date as 24 May 2026. Reach the centre per the shift stated on the admit card, not on any third-party website.
