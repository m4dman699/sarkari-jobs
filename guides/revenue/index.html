How selection works, stage by stage

Each stage below is a real gate. Only the last one leads to an appointment letter.

  1. 1

    Tier 1 (CBT)

    100 Qs, 60 min — Reasoning, GK, Quant, English. 0.50 negative marking.

    Qualifying. Cut-off varies by post and category.

  2. 2

    Tier 2 (CBT)

    200 Qs, 2 hrs — Quant, English, GK, Reasoning. + Descriptive (essay + letter).

    Merit prepared from Tier 2. Descriptive qualifying.

  3. 3

    Physical Test (Customs)

    Running: 1.6km (men) / 800m (women). Long jump, high jump, physical standards.

    Only for Customs Inspector/Havaldar. Qualifying.

  4. 4

    Document Verification

    All originals, caste, NOC. Eye test for Customs (color vision).

    Final appointment after medical fitness.

How to apply, in five steps

Almost every central and state portal follows this order. Complete each step before moving on — most portals stop letting you edit once the fee is paid.

  1. 1

    Read the official notice

    Confirm qualification, age as on the notice's cut-off date, fee and the last date before you register.

  2. 2

    Register on the official portal

    Use the link on the notice, not a search result. Keep the registration number and password it gives you.

  3. 3

    Fill the form, upload photo and signature

    Enter your name exactly as on your Class 10 certificate. Upload the photo and signature in the size and format the portal specifies.

  4. 4

    Pay the fee

    Pay online and save the receipt. If you claim a fee waiver, the category certificate must already be in the prescribed format.

  5. 5

    Download the confirmation

    Save the submitted application PDF and the payment receipt. Check the portal again after a day to confirm the form was accepted.

Documents to keep ready

Scan these before the form opens. A missing certificate is the most common reason an otherwise eligible application is rejected at verification.

  • Passport photograph

    Recent, plain light background, within the portal's file-size limit.

  • Signature

    Signed in black ink on white paper, scanned at the size the portal asks for.

  • Class 10 certificate

    Proves your date of birth and is the usual minimum qualification.

  • Degree or marksheet

    Final or provisional, whichever the notice says it accepts.

  • Caste or EWS certificate

    In the prescribed format from the competent authority, valid on the cut-off date.

  • Domicile or residence proof

    Needed only where the notice reserves posts for a state or region.

  • Photo ID

    Aadhaar, PAN, passport or voter card — the name must match the application.

  • Fee payment proof

    UPI or bank receipt, kept until the admit card is issued.

Exam syllabus & pattern

Official syllabus and exam pattern for this sector. Always verify on the recruiting authority's website.

SSC CGL — Income Tax Inspector / CBIC Inspector

Entry to Inspector posts in CBDT and CBIC is through SSC CGL. Tier 1: 100 questions, 200 marks, 60 minutes, negative 0.50. Tier 2: section-wise papers with negative marking; the syllabus is the CGL syllabus.

  • Tier 1 sections — General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension
  • Tier 2 papers — Mathematical abilities, reasoning and general intelligence, English language and comprehension, general awareness, computer proficiency, and the descriptive paper where applicable
  • Post-specific — CBIC Inspector posts add a physical standard test; CBDT posts do not. Check the CGL notice for the post you are opting

Recommended books: SSC CGL Guide (Amazon) · Flipkart

State Commercial Tax Officer

State PSC or state tax board examination with general studies, law and accounts papers. The exact subjects and marks are set by each state public service commission.

  • General studies — Polity, economy, history, geography, current affairs of the state and the country
  • Tax law — State VAT / GST law, registration, input tax credit, returns, assessment, appeals and penalties
  • Accounts and commerce — Basic accounting, bookkeeping, auditing and commercial arithmetic
Official syllabus →
Recommended books: SSC CGL Tier 2 Guide (Amazon)

Departmental GST / Customs exam

Departmental promotion examinations for existing inspectors and superintendents, conducted by the CBIC and state tax departments on the law in force.

  • Indirect tax law — CGST Act and rules, IGST, customs law, central excise legacy provisions, demand and recovery, refunds, appeals
  • Procedure — Assessment, audit, search and seizure, prosecution, and departmental procedure manuals

When and how to prepare

  1. 1
    If you are entering through CGL

    Prepare the SSC CGL syllabus first — reasoning, quantitative aptitude, English and general awareness. Revenue posts are allotted on the CGL merit and preference form, so the CGL score decides whether you get a tax post at all.

  2. 2
    Months 1–3 — Quant and reasoning base

    Arithmetic, advance maths and reasoning at CGL level. Revenue inspector posts are cut-off driven, and Tier 2 maths carries the most marks.

  3. 3
    Months 3–5 — English and awareness

    English is the fastest section to improve. Add a daily current-affairs slot and a basic economy glossary — budget, GST, direct tax, customs terms appear in general awareness.

  4. 4
    State exams — tax law

    If you are targeting a state commercial tax officer post, read the state VAT/GST Act and the state PSC syllabus. Add basic accountancy; most candidates lose marks there, not in general studies.

  5. 5
    Final phase — Mocks and physicals

    Weekly CGL mocks with the 0.50 negative-marking rule, and for CBIC posts start the physical standard practice early — it is qualifying and non-negotiable.

Career path after joining

Promotions run on seniority plus departmental exams. The rung you enter on depends on the post you clear.

  1. 1 Tax Assistant / Havaldar (Level 3-4)
  2. 2 Inspector (Level 7)
  3. 3 Superintendent (Level 8)
  4. 4 Assistant Commissioner (Level 10)
  5. 5 Commissioner / Chief Commissioner (Level 13-15)

What the notification does not tell you

The parts candidates usually discover too late.

  • SSC CGL is the main route for Inspector posts. IRS via UPSC CSE.

  • Income Tax Inspector: field work (raids, surveys) + office work (assessment).

  • Customs Inspector: airport/seaport/land border duty. Irregular hours.

  • Tax Assistant: desk job, data entry, processing returns. Less field work.

  • Physical standards strict for Customs. Color blindness disqualifies.

  • Salary: Inspector ~₹44,900 (Level 7) + DA + HRA. IRS Officer ~₹56,100 (Level 10).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Income Tax Inspector and Customs Inspector?

Income Tax Inspector: assesses income tax, conducts raids/surveys, office-based. Customs Inspector: customs clearance at ports/airports, anti-smuggling. Different departments (CBDT vs CBIC).

Can a commerce graduate apply for revenue jobs?

Yes. Any graduate can apply for Tax Assistant and Inspector posts via SSC CGL. IRS via UPSC CSE. Commerce background helps but is not mandatory.

What is the age limit for revenue jobs?

Tax Assistant: 18-27 years. Inspector: 18-30 years. IRS: 21-32 years. Age relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD.

Is there negative marking in revenue exams?

Yes. SSC CGL Tier 1: 0.50. Tier 2: 0.25. No negative marking in typing/skill tests.

How many vacancies are there in revenue?

Income Tax: 500-1000 (Inspector) annually. Customs: 300-500. Total revenue vacancies exceed 3000 per year via SSC CGL.

Where do I find official revenue notifications?

ssc.gov.in (SSC CGL), upsc.gov.in (IRS). SarkariJobs+ links the official notification on every listing.
