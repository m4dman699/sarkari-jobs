How selection works, stage by stage

Each stage below is a real gate. Only the last one leads to an appointment letter.

  1. 1

    Written Exam

    CTET: 150 Qs, 150 min. Child Pedagogy, Subject, Language I & II, EVS/Maths.

    Qualifying. 60% to pass. Valid for lifetime (from 2021).

  2. 2

    Interview / Demo

    Teaching demo (10–15 min) + personal interview. Subject knowledge + pedagogy.

    KVS/NVS have interview. State TET recruitments may skip interview (merit-based).

  3. 3

    Document Verification

    Degree, B.Ed, TET certificate, caste, domicile, NOC.

    B.Ed must be from NCTE-recognised institution. Verify before applying.

  4. 4

    Medical & Joining

    Basic medical fitness. Joining in allotted school/region.

    KVS/NVS have transferable posts. State boards: home state preference.

Career path after joining

Promotions run on seniority plus departmental exams. The rung you enter on depends on the post you clear.

  1. 1 Primary Teacher (Level 6)
  2. 2 TGT / PGT (Level 7-8)
  3. 3 Vice Principal / Headmaster (Level 9-10)
  4. 4 Principal (Level 11-12)
  5. 5 Education Officer / DEO (Level 13+)

What the notification does not tell you

The parts candidates usually discover too late.

  • CTET is qualifying only — it doesn't get you a job. You need a recruitment exam (KVS, NVS, state boards) separately.

  • KVS and NVS are central; state boards are state-specific. Apply to both for more chances.

  • B.Ed is mandatory for TGT/PGT. Without B.Ed, only PRT (primary) posts are open.

  • State TET (like UPTET, HTET) is separate from CTET. Some states accept CTET; many require state TET.

  • Teaching jobs have high competition. KVS PGT attracts 50,000+ applicants for 1000 posts.

  • Salary: KVS PGT ~₹47,600 (Level 8) + DA + HRA. State varies. Private schools pay less.

Frequently asked questions

What is CTET and is it mandatory?

Central Teacher Eligibility Test — qualifying exam for teaching Classes 1–8 in central schools (KVS, NVS, Army schools). Mandatory for central recruitment. State schools may require state TET instead.

Can a B.Tech graduate become a teacher?

Yes. B.Tech + B.Ed makes you eligible for TGT (Maths/Science) in KVS, NVS, and state boards. Some states allow B.Tech without B.Ed for specific subjects.

What is the difference between KVS and NVS?

KVS = Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (urban, central government employees' children). NVS = Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (rural, free residential schools). Both central, similar pay, different locations.

How many vacancies are there in teaching?

KVS recruits 5,000–10,000+ annually. NVS recruits 2,000–3,000. State boards vary widely (UP TGT: 10,000+). Total government teaching vacancies exceed 1 lakh per year.

What is the age limit for teaching jobs?

KVS: 18–35 years (PRT), 21–35 (TGT/PGT). NVS: similar. State boards: varies (often 21–40). Age relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD.

Where do I find official teaching notifications?

kvs.gov.in (KVS), navodaya.gov.in (NVS), cbse.gov.in (CTET), and state education boards. SarkariJobs+ links the official notification on every listing.
