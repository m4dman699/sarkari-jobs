How selection works, stage by stage

Open a stage for what it checks. The exact order and tests can vary by notification.

  1. 1

    Written Exam

    CTET: 150 Qs, 150 min. Child Pedagogy, Subject, Language I & II, EVS/Maths.

    Qualifying. 60% to pass. Valid for lifetime (from 2021).

  2. 2

    Interview / Demo

    Teaching demo (10–15 min) + personal interview. Subject knowledge + pedagogy.

    KVS/NVS have interview. State TET recruitments may skip interview (merit-based).

  3. 3

    Document Verification

    Degree, B.Ed, TET certificate, caste, domicile, NOC.

    B.Ed must be from NCTE-recognised institution. Verify before applying.

  4. 4

    Medical & Joining

    Basic medical fitness. Joining in allotted school/region.

    KVS/NVS have transferable posts. State boards: home state preference.

  1. 1

    Read the official notice

    Confirm qualification, age as on the notice's cut-off date, fee and the last date before you register.

  2. 2

    Register on the official portal

    Use the link on the notice, not a search result. Keep the registration number and password it gives you.

  3. 3

    Fill the form, upload photo and signature

    Enter your name exactly as on your Class 10 certificate. Upload the photo and signature in the size and format the portal specifies.

  4. 4

    Pay online and save the receipt. If you claim a fee waiver, the category certificate must already be in the prescribed format.

  5. 5

    Download the confirmation

    Save the submitted application PDF and the payment receipt. Check the portal again after a day to confirm the form was accepted.

Documents to keep ready

Check the official notice for exact formats, dimensions, validity dates and category-specific requirements.

  • Passport photograph

    Recent, plain light background, within the portal's file-size limit.

  • Signature

    Signed in black ink on white paper, scanned at the size the portal asks for.

  • Class 10 certificate

    Proves your date of birth and is the usual minimum qualification.

  • Degree or marksheet

    Final or provisional, whichever the notice says it accepts.

  • Caste or EWS certificate

    In the prescribed format from the competent authority, valid on the cut-off date.

  • Domicile or residence proof

    Needed only where the notice reserves posts for a state or region.

  • Photo ID

    Aadhaar, PAN, passport or voter card — the name must match the application.

  • Fee payment proof

    UPI or bank receipt, kept until the admit card is issued.

Syllabus and preparation

Use official syllabus links where available; verify the current pattern on the recruiting authority’s site.

CTET Paper 1 (Classes 1–5)

150 questions, 150 marks, 150 minutes: Child Development & Pedagogy 30, Language I 30, Language II 30, Mathematics 30, Environmental Studies 30. No negative marking. Qualifying 60% (90/150).

  • Child Development & Pedagogy — Development and its relation to learning, Piaget, Kohlberg and Vygotsky, socialisation, intelligence, language and thought, gender, individual differences, assessment, inclusive education, learning difficulties
  • Mathematics — Geometry, shapes and spatial understanding, numbers, addition and subtraction, multiplication and division, measurement, time, money, patterns, data handling, pedagogical issues
  • Environmental Studies — Family and friends, food, shelter, water, travel, things we make and do, natural resources, pedagogy of EVS
  • Language I and II — Comprehension, pedagogy of language development, grammar, teaching-learning materials, remedial teaching
Official syllabus →
Recommended books: CTET Paper 1 Guide (Amazon) · Flipkart

CTET Paper 2 (Classes 6–8)

150 questions, 150 marks, 150 minutes: CDP 30, Language I 30, Language II 30, and either Mathematics & Science 60 or Social Science 60. Qualifying 60%.

  • Mathematics & Science — Number system, algebra, geometry, mensuration, data handling; food, materials, the world of the living, moving things, how things work, natural phenomena, pedagogy of science
  • Social Science — History (ancient, medieval, modern), geography, social and political life, pedagogy of social science
  • CDP and Languages — Same as Paper 1 but at upper-primary level, with more emphasis on inclusive education and assessment
Official syllabus →
Recommended books: CTET Paper 2 Guide (Amazon)

KVS / NVS — PRT, TGT, PGT

Computer-based written test on subject knowledge, pedagogy, reasoning, general awareness and language, followed by an interview and demo class. Weightage is published in each KVS/NVS notification — read that notice before you prepare.

  • Subject knowledge — The NCERT syllabus of the subject and the classes you are applying to teach
  • Pedagogy — Teaching methods of that subject, lesson planning, evaluation, child psychology
  • Aptitude and language — Reasoning, general awareness, Hindi and English
Official syllabus →
Recommended books: KVS TGT/PGT Guide (Amazon)

State TET

State TETs follow the CTET structure — Paper 1 for Classes 1–5 and Paper 2 for 6–8 — with state-specific qualifying marks. Several states add a state-specific general studies section.

  • Common core — CDP, two languages, mathematics and EVS or science and social science, as in CTET
  • State addition — State GK, state education policy and, in some states, the local language paper

When and how to prepare

  1. 1
    6 months out — Pedagogy first

    CDP and subject pedagogy carry 30–60 marks and are the most ignored part. Read one pedagogy book cover to cover before subject revision; it also supplies the vocabulary the interview expects.

  2. 2
    Months 2–4 — Subject and languages

    Revise the NCERT of the subject and class you are targeting, then pick the two languages you score highest in. Language papers are scoring sections, not filler.

  3. 3
    Month 5 — Previous years

    Solve 10 years of CTET papers. CTET repeats concepts and often the same pedagogy questions. Track which subject gives you the lowest accuracy and give it double time.

  4. 4
    Final month — Full papers

    One full 150-question paper a week at exam timing. No negative marking means you should attempt every question — practise that habit in mocks.

  5. 5
    After the written — Demo class

    KVS, NVS and state interviews include a demo lesson. Practise a 10-minute lesson with a blackboard plan, an activity and a closing question. Record yourself once.

Practical details to verify

These checks vary by recruitment; follow the current notification where it differs.

  • CTET is qualifying only — it doesn't get you a job. You need a recruitment exam (KVS, NVS, state boards) separately.

  • KVS and NVS are central; state boards are state-specific. Apply to both for more chances.

  • B.Ed is mandatory for TGT/PGT. Without B.Ed, only PRT (primary) posts are open.

  • State TET (like UPTET, HTET) is separate from CTET. Some states accept CTET; many require state TET.

  • Teaching jobs have high competition. KVS PGT attracts 50,000+ applicants for 1000 posts.

  • Salary: KVS PGT ~₹47,600 (Level 8) + DA + HRA. State varies. Private schools pay less.

Frequently asked questions
What is CTET and is it mandatory?

Central Teacher Eligibility Test — qualifying exam for teaching Classes 1–8 in central schools (KVS, NVS, Army schools). Mandatory for central recruitment. State schools may require state TET instead.

Can a B.Tech graduate become a teacher?

Yes. B.Tech + B.Ed makes you eligible for TGT (Maths/Science) in KVS, NVS, and state boards. Some states allow B.Tech without B.Ed for specific subjects.

What is the difference between KVS and NVS?

KVS = Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (urban, central government employees' children). NVS = Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (rural, free residential schools). Both central, similar pay, different locations.

How many vacancies are there in teaching?

KVS recruits 5,000–10,000+ annually. NVS recruits 2,000–3,000. State boards vary widely (UP TGT: 10,000+). Total government teaching vacancies exceed 1 lakh per year.

What is the age limit for teaching jobs?

KVS: 18–35 years (PRT), 21–35 (TGT/PGT). NVS: similar. State boards: varies (often 21–40). Age relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD.

Where do I find official teaching notifications?

kvs.gov.in (KVS), navodaya.gov.in (NVS), cbse.gov.in (CTET), and state education boards. SarkariJobs+ links the official notification on every listing.
