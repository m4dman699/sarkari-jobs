How selection works, stage by stage

Open a stage for what it checks. The exact order and tests can vary by notification.

  1. 1

    Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

    Running: 1.6km (men) / 800m (women) in time limit. Long jump, high jump.

    Qualifying. 5–10% pass. Start running 3 months before.

  2. 2

    Physical Standard Test (PST)

    Height: 165cm (men), 152cm (women). Chest: 79-84cm (men). Weight proportional.

    Strict measurement. No relaxation except for specific categories.

  3. 3

    Written Exam

    100-200 Qs. GK, Reasoning, Numerical Ability, General Hindi/English.

    Merit prepared from written score. Negative marking 0.25-0.50.

  4. 4

    Interview / Document Verification

    Personal interview (for SI/Dy.SP). All originals, caste, domicile.

    Final appointment after medical fitness (vision, hearing, BMI).

  1. 1

    Read the official notice

    Confirm qualification, age as on the notice's cut-off date, fee and the last date before you register.

  2. 2

    Register on the official portal

    Use the link on the notice, not a search result. Keep the registration number and password it gives you.

  3. 3

    Fill the form, upload photo and signature

    Enter your name exactly as on your Class 10 certificate. Upload the photo and signature in the size and format the portal specifies.

  4. 4

Documents to keep ready

Check the official notice for exact formats, dimensions, validity dates and category-specific requirements.

  • Passport photograph

    Recent, plain light background, within the portal's file-size limit.

  • Signature

    Signed in black ink on white paper, scanned at the size the portal asks for.

  • Class 10 certificate

    Proves your date of birth and is the usual minimum qualification.

  • Degree or marksheet

    Final or provisional, whichever the notice says it accepts.

  • Caste or EWS certificate

    In the prescribed format from the competent authority, valid on the cut-off date.

  • Domicile or residence proof

    Needed only where the notice reserves posts for a state or region.

  • Photo ID

    Aadhaar, PAN, passport or voter card — the name must match the application.

  • Fee payment proof

    UPI or bank receipt, kept until the admit card is issued.

Syllabus and preparation

Use official syllabus links where available; verify the current pattern on the recruiting authority’s site.

SSC GD Constable

80 questions, 160 marks, 60 minutes: General Intelligence & Reasoning 20, General Knowledge & General Awareness 20, Elementary Mathematics 20, English/Hindi 20. Negative 0.25. Followed by PET/PST and medical.

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning — Analogies, similarities and differences, spatial visualisation, spatial orientation, visual memory, observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasoning, figural classification, arithmetic number series, non-verbal series, coding-decoding, statement-conclusion
  • General Knowledge & Awareness — Current events, India and its neighbours, sports, history, culture, geography, economic scene, polity, scientific research
  • Elementary Mathematics — Number system, whole numbers, decimals and fractions, LCM and HCF, ratio and proportion, percentage, average, profit and loss, discount, mensuration, time and distance, time and work, simple interest
  • English / Hindi — Grammar, vocabulary, sentence structure, synonyms and antonyms, error spotting, comprehension

State police constable (written)

Most state boards use a 100-mark objective paper on reasoning, general studies, mathematics and the state language, followed by a physical efficiency test. Exact marks and negative marking are set by each state board.

  • General Studies — Indian polity and constitution, history, geography, economy, science, current affairs, and state-specific general knowledge which carries heavy weight
  • Reasoning and Mathematics — Series, analogy, coding-decoding, direction, blood relations, percentage, ratio, average, time-work, mensuration
  • Law awareness (SI and some constable papers) — Indian Penal Code basics, CrPC basics, Evidence Act basics, police procedures and human rights

PET / PST — physical test

Typical state standard: 1.6 km run for men and 800 m for women within a fixed time, long jump and high jump, plus height, chest and eyesight standards. Qualifying — but it decides the final list in many states.

  • What is measured — Endurance (run), explosive strength (long and high jump), and body measurements. Standards differ by state and by category — check your state notification

CAPF Assistant Commandant (UPSC)

Paper I 250 marks, 2 hours, objective: general ability and intelligence. Paper II 200 marks, 3 hours, descriptive: essay, precis and report writing. Then PET/PST, medical and interview.

  • Paper I — General mental ability, general science, current events, Indian polity and economy, history, geography, security and law
  • Paper II — Essay, precis writing, report writing, grammar and comprehension

When and how to prepare

  1. 1
    Day one — Physical training

    Start the run, long jump and high jump on day one, not after the written result. Physical tests are qualifying but in many states they also decide the final merit, and fitness cannot be built in a month.

  2. 2
    Months 1–2 — Written basics

    Mathematics and reasoning at Class 10 level plus one general studies book. State police papers are easier than SSC but test state-specific GK heavily — get the state board's own syllabus and previous papers.

  3. 3
    Months 3–4 — Previous papers

    Solve the last 5–10 years of the state constable paper and SSC GD papers. Note repeated questions on state history, festivals, districts and police-related law.

  4. 4
    Month 5 — Mocks and medical

    Weekly full mocks with the negative-marking rule practised (skip only when you cannot eliminate two options). Check eyesight, height, chest and tattoo rules now — they are absolute disqualifications.

  5. 5
    Final month — Combined cycle

    Alternate written revision with physical practice. Run at the same time of day as the PET slot, and do two full timed runs a week to know your pace.

Practical details to verify

These checks vary by recruitment; follow the current notification where it differs.

  • Police recruitment is state-specific. Each state has its own exam, syllabus, and physical standards.

  • Physical test eliminates 80-90% of candidates. Fitness is more important than book knowledge.

  • Constable has no interview — pure merit (written + physical). SI/Dy.SP have interview.

  • Medical standards are strict. Flat feet, knock knees, colour blindness = rejection.

  • Working hours are long and irregular. Night shifts, emergencies, transfers to remote areas.

  • Salary: Constable ~₹21,700 (Level 3) + DA + HRA. SI ~₹35,400 (Level 6) + allowances.

Frequently asked questions
What is the difference between Constable and Sub-Inspector?

Constable: 10th pass, Level 3, beat policing. Sub-Inspector: graduate, Level 6, station house officer with investigation powers. Different exams, different eligibility.

Can women apply for police jobs?

Yes. Women can apply for Constable, Head Constable, SI, and Dy.SP. Separate physical standards for women (lower height, different running distance).

What is the age limit for police recruitment?

Constable: 18-25 years. Head Constable: 18-25. SI: 20-28 years. Dy.SP: 21-30 years. Age relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD.

Is there negative marking in police exams?

Yes. Most state police exams have 0.25-0.50 negative marking per wrong answer. No negative marking in physical tests.

How many vacancies are there in police recruitment?

UP Police: 50,000+ (2024). Bihar Police: 20,000+. Total state police vacancies exceed 2 lakh per year across India.

Where do I find official police notifications?

State police websites (e.g., uppbpb.gov.in, csbc.bih.nic.in). SarkariJobs+ links the official notification on every listing page.
