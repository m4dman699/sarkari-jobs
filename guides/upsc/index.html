How selection works, stage by stage

Each stage below is a real gate. Only the last one leads to an appointment letter.

  1. 1

    Preliminary Exam

    Paper 1: GS (100 Qs, 200 marks, 2 hrs). Paper 2: CSAT (80 Qs, 200 marks, 2 hrs).

    CSAT qualifying (33%). GS score decides cut-off. Negative marking 1/3.

  2. 2

    Main Exam

    9 papers: Essay, GS I-IV, Optional I-II, Language (qualifying). Total 1750 marks.

    Written. 3 hrs per paper. Optional subject chosen by candidate.

  3. 3

    Personality Test (Interview)

    275 marks. Board of 4-5 members. 30-45 min. Personality, current affairs, optional subject.

    No coaching can substitute genuine personality. DAF (Detailed Application Form) is key.

  4. 4

    Final Merit

    Mains (1750) + Interview (275) = 2025 marks. Service allocated by rank + preference.

    Top 900-1000 candidates recommended. Service allocation by merit + preference.

Career path after joining

Promotions run on seniority plus departmental exams. The rung you enter on depends on the post you clear.

  1. 1 Probationer (Level 10, training 2 years)
  2. 2 Under Secretary / SDM / ASP (Level 11)
  3. 3 Deputy Secretary / ADM / SP (Level 12)
  4. 4 Joint Secretary / Commissioner / DIG (Level 13-14)
  5. 5 Secretary / DGP / Ambassador (Level 15-17)

What the notification does not tell you

The parts candidates usually discover too late.

  • UPSC CSE is the most competitive exam in India. 10 lakh+ applicants, ~900 vacancies. Success rate < 0.1%.

  • Preparation takes 1-2 years minimum. Many take 3-4 attempts. Average age of successful candidate: 27-28.

  • Optional subject choice is crucial. Choose based on interest and scoring potential, not popularity.

  • Current affairs matter more than static knowledge. Read The Hindu/Indian Express daily.

  • Interview is not about knowledge — it tests personality, balance, and integrity. DAF preparation is key.

  • Service allocation: IAS > IPS > IFS > IRS (by rank). Top 100 get IAS. Rank 100-200 get IPS/IFS.

Frequently asked questions

How many attempts are allowed for UPSC CSE?

General: 6 attempts (age 21-32). OBC: 9 attempts (age 21-35). SC/ST: unlimited (age 21-37). PwD: 9 attempts (age 21-42).

What is the difference between IAS and IPS?

IAS = administrative service (district collector, policy making). IPS = police service (SP, DIG, DGP). Both are Group A. IAS has higher status and broader scope.

Can I choose my optional subject freely?

Yes. Any subject from the UPSC list. No restriction based on graduation. Choose based on interest, syllabus overlap with GS, and availability of study material.

What is the UPSC interview like?

275 marks. 4-5 member board. 30-45 minutes. Questions on DAF (hobbies, education, work), current affairs, optional subject, and situational judgment. No right/wrong answers — personality assessment.

Is coaching necessary for UPSC?

Not mandatory. Many clear without coaching. Coaching helps with structure and mock tests. Self-study with standard books + test series is equally effective.

Where do I find official UPSC notifications?

upsc.gov.in (main site) and upsconline.nic.in (application). SarkariJobs+ links the official notification PDF on every listing page.
