How selection works, stage by stage

Each stage below is a real gate. Only the last one leads to an appointment letter.

  1. 1

    Tier 1 (CBT)

    100 Qs, 60 min. Reasoning, GK, Quant, English. 200 marks.

    Qualifying. Negative marking 0.50. Cut-off varies by post and category.

  2. 2

    Tier 2 (CBT)

    200 Qs, 2 hrs. Quant, English, GK, Reasoning. + Computer (qualifying).

    Merit prepared from Tier 2. Descriptive paper (essay + letter) for some posts.

  3. 3

    Skill Test / Typing

    Typing: 35 wpm English / 30 wpm Hindi (10 min). DEST for DEO.

    Qualifying only. No marks added to merit.

  4. 4

    Document Verification

    All originals, caste, domicile, NOC, gap certificate.

    Any discrepancy = rejection. Verify before applying.

Career path after joining

Promotions run on seniority plus departmental exams. The rung you enter on depends on the post you clear.

  1. 1 MTS / Constable (Level 1-3)
  2. 2 LDC / DEO / Stenographer (Level 2-6)
  3. 3 Inspector / Sub-Inspector / Auditor (Level 4-8)
  4. 4 Assistant Section Officer (Level 7)
  5. 5 Section Officer / Under Secretary (Level 8-10)

What the notification does not tell you

The parts candidates usually discover too late.

  • SSC CGL is the most popular government exam. 20-30 lakh applicants for 7000-15000 vacancies.

  • Tier 1 is qualifying but the cut-off is high. Focus on accuracy, not just speed.

  • CHSL and CGL have different syllabi. CHSL is easier but fewer vacancies.

  • GD Constable has physical tests (running, long jump, high jump). Start fitness early.

  • SSC exams have a fixed calendar. CGL in Aug-Sep, CHSL in Mar-Apr, MTS in Sep-Oct.

  • Job posting: Income Tax (CBDT) and Customs (CBIC) are most sought after in CGL.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between SSC CGL and SSC CHSL?

CGL = Combined Graduate Level (graduate posts: Inspector, Auditor, ASO). CHSL = Combined Higher Secondary Level (12th pass posts: Clerk, DEO, PA). Different eligibility, different exams.

Can a 12th pass apply for SSC CGL?

No. CGL requires graduation. 12th pass can apply for CHSL, MTS, GD Constable, and Stenographer.

What is the age limit for SSC exams?

MTS: 18-25 years. CHSL: 18-27 years. CGL: 18-30 years (varies by post). CPO: 20-25 years. GD Constable: 18-23 years. Age relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD.

How many attempts are allowed?

No attempt limit for SSC exams (age-based). You can apply as long as you meet the age criteria.

Is there negative marking in SSC exams?

Yes. Tier 1: 0.50 per wrong answer. Tier 2: 0.25 per wrong answer (varies by paper). No negative marking in skill test or typing.

Where do I find official SSC notifications?

ssc.gov.in (main site) and regional SSC websites. SarkariJobs+ links the official notification PDF on every listing page.
