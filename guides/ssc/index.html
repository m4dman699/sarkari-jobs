How selection works, stage by stage

Open a stage for what it checks. The exact order and tests can vary by notification.

  1. 1

    Tier 1 (CBT)

    100 Qs, 60 min. Reasoning, GK, Quant, English. 200 marks.

    Qualifying. Negative marking 0.50. Cut-off varies by post and category.

  2. 2

    Tier 2 (CBT)

    200 Qs, 2 hrs. Quant, English, GK, Reasoning. + Computer (qualifying).

    Merit prepared from Tier 2. Descriptive paper (essay + letter) for some posts.

  3. 3

    Skill Test / Typing

    Typing: 35 wpm English / 30 wpm Hindi (10 min). DEST for DEO.

    Qualifying only. No marks added to merit.

  4. 4

    Document Verification

    All originals, caste, domicile, NOC, gap certificate.

    Any discrepancy = rejection. Verify before applying.

Application checklistApply in five steps Open checklist

Use the recruiting authority’s instructions as the final word. Many portals stop allowing edits after fee payment.

  1. 1

    Read the official notice

    Confirm qualification, age as on the notice's cut-off date, fee and the last date before you register.

  2. 2

    Register on the official portal

    Use the link on the notice, not a search result. Keep the registration number and password it gives you.

  3. 3

    Fill the form, upload photo and signature

    Enter your name exactly as on your Class 10 certificate. Upload the photo and signature in the size and format the portal specifies.

  4. 4

    Pay the fee

    Pay online and save the receipt. If you claim a fee waiver, the category certificate must already be in the prescribed format.

  5. 5

    Download the confirmation

    Save the submitted application PDF and the payment receipt. Check the portal again after a day to confirm the form was accepted.

Documents to keep ready

Check the official notice for exact formats, dimensions, validity dates and category-specific requirements.

  • Passport photograph

    Recent, plain light background, within the portal's file-size limit.

  • Signature

    Signed in black ink on white paper, scanned at the size the portal asks for.

  • Class 10 certificate

    Proves your date of birth and is the usual minimum qualification.

  • Degree or marksheet

    Final or provisional, whichever the notice says it accepts.

  • Caste or EWS certificate

    In the prescribed format from the competent authority, valid on the cut-off date.

  • Domicile or residence proof

    Needed only where the notice reserves posts for a state or region.

  • Photo ID

    Aadhaar, PAN, passport or voter card — the name must match the application.

  • Fee payment proof

    UPI or bank receipt, kept until the admit card is issued.

Syllabus and preparation

Use official syllabus links where available; verify the current pattern on the recruiting authority’s site.

Tier 1 — CGL and CHSL (Computer Based Examination)

100 questions, 200 marks, 60 minutes. Negative marking 0.50 per wrong answer. Section-wise split is published in the notice; the syllabus below is common to both.

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning — Analogies, similarities and differences, space visualisation, problem solving, analysis, judgement, decision making, visual memory, discrimination, observation, arithmetical reasoning, verbal and figure classification, non-verbal series, coding-decoding, statement-conclusion, syllogistic reasoning
  • Quantitative Aptitude (Arithmetic) — Whole numbers, decimals, fractions, percentage, ratio and proportion, square roots, averages, interest, profit and loss, discount, partnership, mixture, time and distance, time and work, basic algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, height and distance, data interpretation, charts and graphs
  • General Awareness — India and its neighbouring countries, history, culture, geography, economic scene, general polity, Indian constitution, scientific developments, current affairs
  • English Comprehension — Spot the error, fill in the blanks, synonyms and antonyms, spelling correction, idioms and phrases, one word substitution, active and passive voice, direct and indirect narration, cloze passage, reading comprehension, sentence improvement, shuffling of sentence parts, sentence rearrangement

Tier 2 — CGL (Mains)

Objective papers held in sessions as published in the notice, with negative marking. A descriptive paper (essay and letter) is qualifying for some posts.

  • Mathematical Abilities — Arithmetic at higher difficulty, advanced mensuration, geometry, trigonometry, surds and indices, statistics, data interpretation, charts and tables
  • Reasoning and General Intelligence — Higher-difficulty puzzles, seating arrangement, blood relations, syllogism, coding-decoding, data sufficiency
  • English Language & Comprehension — Vocabulary, grammar and usage, error spotting, cloze test, reading comprehension, para jumbles, sentence rearrangement
  • General Awareness — Deeper current affairs, history and culture, polity and constitution, economy, science and technology, environment, computers
  • Computer Proficiency — Computer basics, MS Office, operating systems, networking, internet and email — qualifying

Skill Test / Typing / DEST

Typing: 35 wpm English or 30 wpm Hindi in 10 minutes. Data Entry Speed Test (DEST) for DEO posts. Both qualifying.

When and how to prepare

  1. 1
    Start the notice is out — Months 1–2

    Give one diagnostic Tier 1 mock cold on day one. That tells you which of the four sections costs you the most marks. Start with that section for the first month; do not spend equal time on all four.

  2. 2
    Months 3–4 — Section-wise completion

    Finish English grammar and vocabulary (the highest-scoring, lowest-cost section), then reasoning type by type. Do 50 arithmetic questions daily — SSC is mostly speed maths, not advanced maths.

  3. 3
    Month 5 — Mock-led

    Two Tier 1 mocks a week and one Tier 2 section test. Review every wrong answer by writing it in an error notebook. Target 45 seconds per question.

  4. 4
    Month 6 — Tier 2 focus

    If the exam calendar puts Tier 2 soon after Tier 1, start Tier 2 preparation in parallel from month 3. Tier 2 is where final merit is decided — Tier 1 only filters.

  5. 5
    Final 2 weeks — Timing and revision

    Full mocks at the actual slot, error notebook revision, and 10 minutes of typing practice daily for the skill test. Do not open new books.

Practical details to verify

These checks vary by recruitment; follow the current notification where it differs.

  • SSC CGL is the most popular government exam. 20-30 lakh applicants for 7000-15000 vacancies.

  • Tier 1 is qualifying but the cut-off is high. Focus on accuracy, not just speed.

  • CHSL and CGL have different syllabi. CHSL is easier but fewer vacancies.

  • GD Constable has physical tests (running, long jump, high jump). Start fitness early.

  • SSC exams have a fixed calendar. CGL in Aug-Sep, CHSL in Mar-Apr, MTS in Sep-Oct.

  • Job posting: Income Tax (CBDT) and Customs (CBIC) are most sought after in CGL.

Frequently asked questions
What is the difference between SSC CGL and SSC CHSL?

CGL = Combined Graduate Level (graduate posts: Inspector, Auditor, ASO). CHSL = Combined Higher Secondary Level (12th pass posts: Clerk, DEO, PA). Different eligibility, different exams.

Can a 12th pass apply for SSC CGL?

No. CGL requires graduation. 12th pass can apply for CHSL, MTS, GD Constable, and Stenographer.

What is the age limit for SSC exams?

MTS: 18-25 years. CHSL: 18-27 years. CGL: 18-30 years (varies by post). CPO: 20-25 years. GD Constable: 18-23 years. Age relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD.

How many attempts are allowed?

No attempt limit for SSC exams (age-based). You can apply as long as you meet the age criteria.

Is there negative marking in SSC exams?

Yes. Tier 1: 0.50 per wrong answer. Tier 2: 0.25 per wrong answer (varies by paper). No negative marking in skill test or typing.

Where do I find official SSC notifications?

ssc.gov.in (main site) and regional SSC websites. SarkariJobs+ links the official notification PDF on every listing page.
