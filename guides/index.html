How these guides are built

Post names, pay levels and selection stages are summarised from the recruiting authorities' published notifications. Dates, vacancy counts and fees are not typed by hand — they are read from the feed on every build and linked to the official page. Where a notification is silent on something, the field is left empty rather than guessed.
