How selection works, stage by stage

Open a stage for what it checks. The exact order and tests can vary by notification.

  1. 1

    NEET PG / NEET SS

    NEET PG: 200 Qs, 3.5 hrs. NEET SS: 100 Qs, 1.5 hrs.

    For MD/MS/super-specialty. Score determines college allocation.

  2. 2

    Written Exam (for non-PG posts)

    100-200 Qs. Medical knowledge, GK, Reasoning. For Staff Nurse, Pharmacist, etc.

    State-level exams. Some central (AIIMS, ESIC) have own exams.

  3. 3

    Interview / Skill Test

    Personal interview. For MO posts: clinical knowledge + personality.

    Some posts have skill test (nursing practical, pharmacy practical).

  4. 4

    Document Verification

    Degree, internship certificate, registration, caste, NOC.

    Medical registration (MCI/NMC) mandatory. Verify before applying.

Application checklistApply in five steps Open checklist

Use the recruiting authority’s instructions as the final word. Many portals stop allowing edits after fee payment.

  1. 1

    Read the official notice

    Confirm qualification, age as on the notice's cut-off date, fee and the last date before you register.

  2. 2

    Register on the official portal

    Use the link on the notice, not a search result. Keep the registration number and password it gives you.

  3. 3

    Fill the form, upload photo and signature

    Enter your name exactly as on your Class 10 certificate. Upload the photo and signature in the size and format the portal specifies.

  4. 4

    Pay the fee

    Pay online and save the receipt. If you claim a fee waiver, the category certificate must already be in the prescribed format.

  5. 5

    Download the confirmation

    Save the submitted application PDF and the payment receipt. Check the portal again after a day to confirm the form was accepted.

Documents to keep ready

Check the official notice for exact formats, dimensions, validity dates and category-specific requirements.

  • Passport photograph

    Recent, plain light background, within the portal's file-size limit.

  • Signature

    Signed in black ink on white paper, scanned at the size the portal asks for.

  • Class 10 certificate

    Proves your date of birth and is the usual minimum qualification.

  • Degree or marksheet

    Final or provisional, whichever the notice says it accepts.

  • Caste or EWS certificate

    In the prescribed format from the competent authority, valid on the cut-off date.

  • Domicile or residence proof

    Needed only where the notice reserves posts for a state or region.

  • Photo ID

    Aadhaar, PAN, passport or voter card — the name must match the application.

  • Fee payment proof

    UPI or bank receipt, kept until the admit card is issued.

Syllabus and preparation

Use official syllabus links where available; verify the current pattern on the recruiting authority’s site.

NEET UG

180 questions, 720 marks, 200 minutes: Physics 45, Chemistry 45, Botany 45, Zoology 45. Each question is 4 marks with a 1-mark penalty for a wrong answer. Required for MBBS, BDS, BSc Nursing and allied courses.

  • Physics — Class 11 and 12 NCERT — mechanics, thermodynamics, oscillations and waves, electrostatics, current electricity, magnetism, optics, modern physics, electronic devices
  • Chemistry — Physical, organic and inorganic chemistry of Class 11 and 12 — mole concept, equilibrium, thermodynamics, electrochemistry, hydrocarbons, biomolecules, coordination compounds, p-block
  • Botany — Diversity in living world, plant physiology, cell structure and function, genetics and evolution, ecology and environment, biotechnology
  • Zoology — Human physiology, reproduction, genetics, evolution, human health and disease, biotechnology applications
Official syllabus →
Recommended books: NEET PG Guide (Amazon) · Flipkart

NORCET — AIIMS Nursing Officer

Computer-based test on nursing subjects, general knowledge and aptitude, held in stages as published in each NORCET notice. Shortlisting is entirely CBT-based, with no interview.

  • Nursing — Fundamentals of nursing, medical-surgical nursing, community health nursing, child health, mental health, midwifery and gynaecology, nursing management
  • General sections — Anatomy, physiology, microbiology, pharmacology, nutrition, general knowledge and current affairs, reasoning and aptitude
Official syllabus →
Recommended books: NORCET Guide (Amazon)

State / ESIC Staff Nurse

State nursing officer and ESIC recruitment use a subject-based objective paper on nursing fundamentals, anatomy, physiology, community health and current affairs, followed by document verification. Marks and negative marking vary by state.

  • Nursing subjects — As per the state nursing council syllabus — fundamentals, medical-surgical, community, maternal and child health
  • Supporting subjects — Anatomy, physiology, microbiology, nutrition, first aid, hospital procedures
Official syllabus →
Recommended books: CMS Guide (Amazon)

Medical Officer (NHM / state health)

Written test or walk-in interview on clinical subjects, public health and the state health programme, as stated in the state notification. Some states use NEET PG or a state-conducted screening test.

  • Clinical — General medicine, surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology, paediatrics, community medicine
  • Public health — National health programmes, immunisation, maternal and child health, health management information system

When and how to prepare

  1. 1
    NEET — Two years, NCERT first

    NEET is an NCERT exam. Read NCERT Biology line by line, then Physics and Chemistry. Finish the syllabus once before joining any test series; mock scores before completion only demoralise.

  2. 2
    NEET — Practice over reading

    From the second half, 100 MCQs a day and one full paper a week. Biology decides the rank — attempt all 90 biology questions and keep a diagram-based revision sheet.

  3. 3
    Nursing exams — Subject revision

    NORCET and state nurse papers reward subject accuracy, not general knowledge. Revise fundamentals and medical-surgical nursing from one standard textbook, then solve previous NORCET papers.

  4. 4
    Nursing exams — Current affairs and aptitude

    Add 20 minutes of health current affairs and 30 minutes of reasoning daily. These sections separate candidates with similar nursing scores.

  5. 5
    Before applying — Registration check

    Confirm your state nursing council registration and eligibility date. Many candidates clear the exam and are rejected at document verification for a lapsed or out-of-state registration.

Career path after joining

Progression depends on cadre, service rules, seniority and departmental exams. Confirm details for the specific post.

  1. 1 Staff Nurse / Pharmacist / Technician (Level 4-7)
  2. 2 Medical Officer / Dental Surgeon (Level 10)
  3. 3 Specialist / Senior Resident (Level 11)
  4. 4 Professor / Head of Department (Level 12-14)
  5. 5 Director / Dean (Level 15+)

Practical details to verify

These checks vary by recruitment; follow the current notification where it differs.

  • AIIMS and central institutes pay better than state hospitals. Prefer central if possible.

  • Staff Nurse (GNM) vs Nursing Officer (B.Sc Nursing): different pay levels and eligibility.

  • NEET PG cut-off is very high. General category needs 50th percentile minimum.

  • Government medical jobs have bond service (1-3 years) in many states. Check before applying.

  • Rural service is often mandatory for new MOs. Remote posting for 1-2 years.

  • Salary: MO ~₹56,100 (Level 10) + NPA (Non-Practicing Allowance). Nursing Officer ~₹44,900 (Level 7).

Frequently asked questions
What is the difference between Staff Nurse and Nursing Officer?

Staff Nurse: GNM diploma, Level 7. Nursing Officer: B.Sc Nursing, Level 7 (higher entry pay). Both do patient care. Nursing Officer has better promotion prospects.

Can a BAMS/BHMS graduate apply for allopathy MO posts?

No. Allopathy MO requires MBBS. AYUSH graduates apply for AYUSH MO posts (BAMS/BHMS/BUMS). Separate exams and vacancies.

What is NEET PG and is it mandatory?

National Eligibility cum Entrance Test for Post-Graduation. Mandatory for MD/MS admission in government and private colleges. Score determines college and specialty allocation.

What is the age limit for medical government jobs?

MO: 21-35 years. Staff Nurse: 18-30 years. Pharmacist: 18-30 years. Age relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD.

Is there negative marking in medical exams?

Yes. NEET PG: 4 marks correct, -1 wrong. AIIMS: 1 mark correct, -1/3 wrong. UPSC CMS: 1 mark correct, -1/3 wrong.

Where do I find official medical job notifications?

aiims.edu (AIIMS), upsc.gov.in (CMS), state health departments, esic.nic.in. SarkariJobs+ links the official notification on every listing.
