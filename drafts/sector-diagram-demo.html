Where a government job can come from

Eight recruiting systems. Each has its own exam, calendar and eligibility floor.

What each qualification actually opens

The same ladder, read bottom-up. This is the part most "jobs after 12th" articles get wrong.

10th

Group D, MTS, GDS, Agniveer (Army), state police constable. Lowest entry bar, highest competition — and the only rung where "no degree, no diploma" is normal.

12th

CHSL, NTPC, Postman, NDA (PCM for Navy/Air Force), Coast Guard Navik, state clerk posts. The widest single jump in options.

ITI / Diploma

ALP, technician and apprentice posts in Railways and PSUs. Trade certificate replaces the degree requirement for technical lines.

Graduate

CGL, IBPS PO/Clerk, state PSC, teaching (with B.Ed/D.El.Ed). Banking clerical and officer roles sit here — not at 12th.
