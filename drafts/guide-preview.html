What growth looks like over the years

A realistic 90-day preparation plan

  1. Days 1–15 — map the target. Pick two routes at most: one "safe" lower-body exam, one ambitious. Read one official notification of each fully once — syllabus lists are inside.
  2. Days 16–45 — basics on repeat. One subject block daily: quantitative + reasoning on alternate days, GK/current-affairs in short daily sessions, language practice in the exam's medium.
  3. Days 46–70 — practice under clock. Timed sectional sets, then one full mock weekly. Review every wrong answer — the mock analysis matters more than the score.
  4. Days 71–90 — exam-window drilling. Official pattern papers, previous-year sets, and revision capsules. Keep a document file ready: photo, signatures, category certificates.
