Contact SarkariJobs+

🐙 Public issue tracker🧾 No documents or money, ever🔄 Reviewed between daily updates
ℹ️

Application, hall-ticket and result problems go to the recruiting authority. SarkariJobs+ is an information site — we cannot see, fix or change anything on an official system, and we never handle applications, fees or documents on anyone's behalf.

One channel, by design

All correspondence runs through a single public issue tracker on GitHub. Every report stays visible and auditable, other readers can confirm the same problem, and there is no private inbox that an impersonator could fake. It is the only official channel for this website.

Open the public issue tracker ↗

Report a listing correction

Wrong date, broken or mislabelled link, a vacancy figure that changed, or a listing that was officially withdrawn? Open an issue with the heading “Correction” and tell us:

  • The URL of the SarkariJobs+ listing page.
  • What is wrong, and what the correct value is.
  • A link to the official notice or portal page that proves it.

Verified fixes go out in a scheduled update — the tracker processes corrections between the daily collection runs, so most fixes land within a day.

Privacy and removal requests

If a public notice lists personal details about you, open an issue titled “Privacy request”, name the page, and explain what should be reviewed. Requests to correct or remove content on privacy grounds are handled against the official record, described in the privacy policy.

Copyright and takedown

Our pages are original summaries with source attribution, as set out in the editorial and content policy. Rights holders: send a takedown request titled “Takedown” naming the page, the work concerned and your rights basis. Valid requests are acted on promptly while verification is under way.

What to send — and what to never send

⚠️

Never include passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar/PAN or bank details, fee receipts, uploaded documents, résumés or anything else you would not publish publicly. Issue reports are public, and no legitimate correction needs your private information.

Only the listing URL, the fact in question and an official link are needed. That is always enough.

Beware of impersonation

We do not call, WhatsApp, email or DM readers about vacancies, and we never charge for information, “processing” or applications. Anyone contacting you claiming to be SarkariJobs+ staff and asking for money or documents is not us — please treat it as fraud.

Site information

Background on the project is on the about page; how records are sourced and corrected is in the editorial policy; and reader data practices are in the privacy policy.
